Free online Planning Poker for agile teams

OpenEstimate is a fast, good-looking Scrum Poker app for sprint planning and story point estimation. Create a room, send the link to your team and start estimating – without accounts, ads or tracking. Votes go straight from browser to browser, so nothing ever lands on a server.

Rooms in one click

Create a room and invite your team with a link or room code. No sign-up for anyone.

All the usual decks

Fibonacci, modified Fibonacci, T-shirt sizes and powers of 2 – plus “?” and coffee break.

Fair, hidden voting

Cards stay face down until someone reveals them for everybody at the same time.

Instant results

Average, suggested card, agreement and vote distribution right after the reveal.

Private by design

Peer-to-peer via WebRTC. No backend, no database, no analytics, no cookies.

Works everywhere

Desktop or phone, light or dark mode, English or German – right in the browser.

How it works

  1. Create a room and pick a card deck.
  2. Share the invite link with your team.
  3. Vote, reveal, discuss – then start the next round.

Frequently asked questions

Is OpenEstimate really free?

Yes. It is completely free and open source – no paid plans, no ads, no limits on rooms or participants.

Do I need an account?

No. Enter a name, create a room and share the invite link. Nobody needs to sign up.

Is my data stored or tracked?

No. There is no backend, no database, no analytics and no cookies. Votes travel directly between the participants' browsers via WebRTC. Details are in the privacy policy.

How many people can join a room?

Typical scrum teams of up to about 15–20 people work well. Because everyone connects directly, very large groups depend on the participants' browsers and networks.

What is Planning Poker?

Planning Poker (also called Scrum Poker) is a consensus-based technique for estimating effort in agile teams. Everyone picks a card in secret, all cards are revealed at once and differences are discussed.
